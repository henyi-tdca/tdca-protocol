# TDCA-SPEC-REGISTER-OF-RIGHTS-001 · **TDCA Registration System** (English Human-Readable Version · `V1.0-FROZEN (IN FORCE)`)


---

## 0. One-line summary

This draft **transcribes the Chinese human-readable version article by article** ( `8` chapters · **`24`** articles ＋ **`3`** annexes): **binary registration** ( ownership registration = confirming rights attribution · authorization registration = determining authority and scenario) ｜ the ownership entry **is broad** ( anything with a form of expression may be registered) while the authorization entry **is strict** ( three requirements · fail-closed) ｜ the **authorization instrument** is uniform in form ( special and custom terms **are agreed by smart contract**) ｜ **compliance-first** ( register and authorize before configuration) ｜ exceptions **only in sandbox or drill**, **leaving the sandbox requires authorization** ｜ **NOT IN FORCE** ( pending four steps ＋ sign-off). This side **does not decide** any threshold, weight, bar, scale, rate or signing authority.

## 1. General provisions

**Article 1 (Purpose)** In order to regulate **registration activities** within the TDCA ecosystem, so that **ownership** and **authorization** are each properly ordered, verifiable and queryable, and so that **configuration (invocation)** always has an instrument, this system is enacted.

**Article 2 (Scope)** This system applies to the **registration of all assets** within the TDCA ecosystem and to their **configuration-admission** activities.

**Article 3 (Basis)** Per registered instruments: *TDCA Metafunction–Interpretant Relation Reconciliation Specification* (** configuration right = the relation between metafunction and interpretant**) ｜ `terminology item` (** minimum configuration unit = scenario** · internal elements **do not participate in configuration directly**) ｜ `terminology item` (** five-tier generalized utility right**) ｜ *IP Copyright Asset Valuation Management Measures* ( registered regulation) ｜ the three new-baseline institutional instruments.

**Article 4 (Basic principles)**
1. **Compliance first**: register first, authorize first, configure afterwards ( **no inversion of order**).
2. **Binary separation**: **ownership registration** and **authorization registration** **are two matters and two ledgers**.
3. **Configuration by instrument**: configuration **always requires an authorization instrument** ( **whether the asset is one's own or another's**).
4. **Exceptions do not cross the cabinet**: exceptions apply **inside sandbox or drill**; **once out of the sandbox an authorization is required**.

---

## 2. Binary registration

**Article 5 (Registration split)**

| | **Ownership registration** | **Authorization registration** |
|---|---|---|
| What it governs | **Rights confirmation** — confirming **attribution of rights** ("who owns this") | **Authority and scenario** — confirming **who may use it, with what authority, in what scenario** |
| Layer | ownership layer | configuration-right layer |
| Entry | **anything with a form of expression may be registered** ( broad) | **three requirements complete** ( strict · see Article 11) |

**Article 6 (Order)** **confirm rights first → then authorize (instrument required) → only then configure**; if any link is missing ⟹ **configuration is not permitted**.

---

## 3. Ownership registration (rights confirmation)

**Article 7 (Entry criterion)** **Anything that has a form of expression may be registered.**

**Article 8 (Assignment of legal domain)**
1. Anything with a **form of expression** ⟹ **governed by copyright**.
2. An expression that is an **invention** ⟹ **governed by patent law**.
3. An expression that is a **digital structuring** ⟹ **governed by data law**.
4. ( a mark) ⟹ governed by **trademark law**.

**Article 9 (Distinction from valuation)** Ownership registration **is identification** ( determining nature and position); a **valuation-capable subject** ( content that can be uniquely marked) **is measurement** —— **the two are at different layers and must not be conflated**.

---

## 4. Authorization registration (determining authority and scenario)

**Article 10 (Content)** Authorization registration **determines authority and scenario** ( who, with what authority, in what scenario).

**Article 11 (Three requirements for validity)**
1. **Scenario constraint** ( scenario mandatory).
2. **Form of the five-tier asset** ( including **smart contracts** and the like).
3. **Full composition authorized** —— missing any one ⟹ **registration is refused** ( **fail-closed**).

**Article 12 (Authorization instrument)**
1. **All of them are authorization instruments** ( **no distinction** between a "self-authorization instrument" and an "other-authorization instrument").
2. **The format is the same**: **a fixed format applies**.
3. **Special terms and custom terms** ⟹ **are all agreed by smart contract**.

**Article 13 (Own assets also require an instrument)** **Whether ownership is one's own or another's**, authorization registration **must have an authorization instrument**; **without the instrument configuration is not permitted** ( "it is mine" **≠ "it may be configured"**).

---

## 5. Configuration admission

**Article 14 (Interpretant and authorization instrument)** The metafunction's **interpretants are all constituted by authorization-registration instruments**; **without an authorization-registration instrument ⟹ no invocation and no configuration**.

**Article 15 (Exceptions)**
1. **Inside the sandbox**: **exception** ( instrument waived).
2. **Drill**: **exception** ( instrument waived).
3. **Leaving the sandbox**: **authorization must be obtained** —— **no leaving the sandbox without authorization**; configuration after leaving **returns to the normal track** ( instrument required).

**Article 16 (Instrument of configuration)** The instrument of configuration ＝ **the authorization-registration instrument** ( the **reduction to an instrument** of "configuration right = the relation between metafunction and interpretant").

---

## 6. Registration review (compliance-first)

**Article 17 (Review at metafunction registration)** **At the registration of a metafunction**, the interpretants' ① **ownership** and ② **authorization tier** **are subject to compliance review**; registration is permitted only after the review passes.

**Article 18 (Effect)** **Compliance review at the registration stage** replaces ex-post stocktaking; **no separate "interpretant inventory" needs to be established**.

---

## 7. Responsibility and exceptions

**Article 19 (Nature of unauthorized configuration)** **Configuration without authorization ＝ infringement**.

**Article 20 (Effect of compliance-first)** Because of **compliance-first**, infringement **is not expected to occur** ( except where **the system malfunctions**); system anomalies are routed to the **exception-handling** line.

---

## 8. Supplementary provisions

**Article 21 (Languages and versions)** This system **requires human and machine versions** ( Chinese / English **human-readable** ＋ **machine-readable**), **with the same version number**, **the human-readable version being the semantic authority**.

**Article 22 (Procedure)** This system follows the registered **four-step institutional-promotion procedure**; where hard constraints are involved **a draft may not be treated as effective**.

**Article 23 (Amendment)** Amendments go through **amending instruments** ( **no rewrite of earlier text**); matters involving red lines and the do-not-decide list **require the founder's highest-authority instruction**.

**Article 24 (Effectiveness)** This system **takes effect on the date of the founder's sign-off**; the scope and batch of effectiveness **are reserved to the founder**.
