# TDCA Cluster-Orchestration Experiment — Reproduction Note

> Goal: let any party **re-run independently** and obtain comparable, readable results.
> Experimental — results **must not** be cited as evidence of institutional effectiveness.

## 1. Environment
Fully **local**; no network required. Record the orchestration tool and version, OS and time zone.
Role isolation within a group is the key prerequisite.

## 2. Preparation
Pick one fixed task (same prompt for A/B/C); prepare injected items and **fix their count and
source** (denominator of J-2); define the required artifact count per round (denominator of J-1).

## 3. Procedure
- A: one task, four orchestrations × 3 rounds (bare; +attestation; +attribution; +circuit-breaker).
- B: one four-role walkthrough (research → critique → adjudication → recording), critique and
  adjudication in **separate** contexts; tabulated output; traceable findings.
- C: three mutually unaware sub-agents on the same task; tally agreements and disagreements.

## 4. Record & compute
Log start/end per round (local + UTC); compute J-1…J-4 with numerator/denominator stated; keep all
intermediate artifacts; label everything SIMULATED.

## 5. Report template
| Group | Round | Artifacts | J-1 | J-2 | J-3 | J-4 | Window | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Plus the artifact inventory and the 4-item declaration.

## 6. Reproducibility
Record random seeds; identical input should yield identical output. Retain failed/aborted rounds
as-is.

## 7. Disclaimer
Experimental method. **Must not** be cited as evidence of institutional effectiveness, nor as
evidence of any real-world effect.
